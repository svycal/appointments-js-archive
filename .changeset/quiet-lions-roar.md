**New:** -- Added webhook events for the appointment meeting lifecycle: `appointment.meeting.created`, `appointment.meeting.updated`, `appointment.meeting.canceled`, and `appointment.meeting.replaced`, plus terminal-failure events `appointment.meeting.create_failed`, `appointment.meeting.update_failed`, `appointment.meeting.cancel_failed`, and `appointment.meeting.replace_failed`. See [AppointmentMeetingCreatedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingcreatedeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingUpdatedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingupdatedeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingCanceledEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingcanceledeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingReplacedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingreplacedeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingCreateFailedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingcreatefailedeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingUpdateFailedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingupdatefailedeventdata), [AppointmentMeetingCancelFailedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingcancelfailedeventdata), and [AppointmentMeetingReplaceFailedEventData](https://developers.savvycal.app/api/schemas/appointmentmeetingreplacefailedeventdata).
